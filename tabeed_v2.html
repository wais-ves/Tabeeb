About Tabeeb

Your AI-powered health intelligence workspace.

Tabeeb is a demonstration of a modern AI-native desktop application. It simulates intelligent analysis of health-related queries, with a focus on clean design, smooth interactions, and persistent data management.

Features: AI Workspace, History, Saved Results, Search, Settings with theme toggle, keyboard shortcuts, and local storage persistence.

Built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
